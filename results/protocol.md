# Detection protocol (Workstream A): split-conformal thresholds, commissioning sweep, environment scan

Per-campaign alarm rates at a split-conformal threshold (alpha = 0.05) from commissioning campaigns. TF = triggered faulty (catch rate), TH/UH = triggered/untriggered healthy (false alarm), UF = untriggered faulty (flag on faults away from the trigger). f_ov = fraction of commissioning inside the trigger region (0.23 = full envelope), f_sh = faulty share of commissioning. Cells are mean over seeds (n given). v3 = Kaggle ramp runs (post-hoc), v2 = pre-existing runs. Scan = single-twin environment scan (permutation p, 500 permutations, minimum 0.002).
